## Definition

Each [Categorized Social Volume](/metrics/social-volume-categorized) metric counts documents from Twitter accounts assigned to one defined category.
There are seven categories: BTC Maxi, Builder, KOL, Media, Memecoin, Trader, and Trading Firm.

---

## How Accounts Are Classified

Accounts are classified into categories by an LLM (gpt-5-mini).

As of the current classification run, 18,203 Twitter accounts have been categorized, based on account activity between `2025-03-09` and `2026-03-09`.

An account can belong to more than one category at a time.

There are also accounts that don't belong to any category, whole volume is only reflected in the regular social volume metric.

Classification runs over a relative historical window.
We use the last 12 months of data or the last 20 X.com posts for newer accounts.
Currently classification is not re-run for already classified accounts; for newly added accounts it runs at the moment we follow the account.

## Categories

### BTC Maxi

Focuses almost exclusively on Bitcoin, promoting BTC dominance, sound money ideology, and dismissing altcoins or memecoins.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `btc_maxi`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_btc_maxi`.

---

### Builder

A developer or technical contributor actively involved in building or maintaining crypto products or protocols.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `builder`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_builder`.

---

### KOL

An influential individual shaping opinions through strong market views or ideological perspectives.
A KOL is defined as an account with 10,000 or more followers.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `kol`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_kol`.

---

### Media

Crypto-focused journalism, content creation, and educational channels.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `media`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_media`.

---

### Memecoin

Centers on memecoins, hype-driven narratives, viral trends, and short-term speculation, often using humor and FOMO.
A Memecoin account is always dedicated to promoting one specific coin, excluding the majors.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `memecoins`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_memecoins`.

---

### Trader

Focuses on short-term market analysis and trading activity, emphasizing charts, technical setups, and signals.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `trader`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_trader`.

---

### Trading Firm

Professional trading entities focused on market making or proprietary strategies.

In the metric name, this category is typed as `trading_firm`.
Example: `social_volume_twitter_trading_firm`.

---

## History

All seven categories share the same original classification window: `2025-03-09` to `2026-03-09`.
