## Overview

Santiment API Plans provide access to various metrics through the API endpoint: [https://api.santiment.net](https://api.santiment.net?utm_source=academy).
Check the [pricing page](https://app.santiment.net/pricing?utm_source=academy) for the latest plans and pricing.

> Note: Some metrics and metric versions are not included in every plan:
>
> - **Beta metrics** (for example, [Categorized Social Volume](/metrics/social-volume-categorized): `social_volume_twitter_kol`, `_trader`, `_media`, `_builder`, ...) are available only to alpha and beta users, on every plan including Business Max. `getAccessRestrictions` still reports them as accessible.
> - **Newer metric versions** need a higher plan: `version: "modern:v1"` needs Business Pro or Business Max, and `version: "modern_pit:v1"` needs a paid yearly Business Max plan. See [Metric Versions](/sanapi/metric-versions#version-access-by-plan).
